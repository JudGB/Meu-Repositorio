Portifolio

“Run! Doctor Need Help” was a game developed for a school project about blood types, where my group decided to transform the subject into an educational digital game. In the game, the player must dodge obstacles inside a precarious hospital while collecting the correct blood bags for different patients; if the wrong blood type is chosen, the patient dies and the game technically explains the reason based on research conducted by the team. The entire project was developed in the GameMaker Studio 2 engine, with programming, concept design, and artwork all created by me.

“Arrows Running” is a game I developed in 2024 for a competition (game jam) whose objective was to create a simple game themed around weapons such as swords, spears, and bows. That is the premise of the game: you are an arrow traveling as far as possible through the map while the difficulty continuously increases. The game was developed in GameMaker in just one day, with all the artwork, ideas, and programming created entirely by me.
